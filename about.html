Our Story

Born on a university campus with a simple dream — to serve the finest cup of coffee and create a space where ideas flourish over every sip.

How It Began

Passion in Every Bean

BEST Coffee started in 2014 as a tiny counter at Chitkara University's Square 1. What began as a passion project between two friends quickly grew into the campus's most beloved gathering spot.

We source our beans directly from small farms in Ethiopia, Coorg, and Colombia — ensuring both quality and fair pay for the farmers who make it all possible.

Every roast, every blend, every cup carries that original spirit: honest craft, genuine warmth, and an obsession with flavour.

Our Philosophy

Slow Coffee, Fast Smiles

We believe good coffee should never be rushed. Our baristas are trained to treat every order as a craft, not a transaction. We steam, pull, and pour with intention.

From our seasonal specials to the daily espresso, every item on our menu is tested rigorously before it earns a place in your cup.
